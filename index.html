Bring a friend

Make a Friend, Be a Friend, Bring Your Friend to Christ

Cursillo has always spread from one friend to another. Share it with someone who would be glad to hear about it.

  1. 1

    Make a friend

    Notice the person nearby — at your parish, at work, next door — especially the one nobody else notices.

  2. 2

    Be a friend

    Walk with them. Listen. Share your own story, simply and without pressure.

  3. 3

    Bring your friend to Christ

    When the time is right, invite them to a weekend in your diocese — and keep them in your palanca.
